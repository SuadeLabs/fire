# theta

---

The **theta** attribute measures the sensitivity of a derivative's value to the passage of time and is a numeric field.

The [Options Industry Council explanation of theta, opening paragraphs][theta] describes time decay with other pricing factors held constant. The rate of decay can vary as expiry approaches. FIRE stores this time sensitivity separately from the derivative value.
