# rho

---

The **rho** attribute measures the sensitivity of the derivative's value to changes in the relevant interest-rate curve or curves and is a numeric field.

The [Options Industry Council explanation of rho, opening paragraph][rho] describes interest-rate sensitivity for options. In the EU prudential framework, [Regulation (EU) 528/2014, recital 3][regulation] also identifies interest-rate risk as rho risk. FIRE stores the rate sensitivity used for the derivative.
