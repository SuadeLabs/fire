# gamma

---

The **gamma** attribute measures the rate of change of delta as the underlying risk factor moves and is a numeric field.

For options and warrants, [Regulation (EU) 528/2014, Article 4(1)(a)][gamma] describes the sensitivity of delta to the underlying price (or the specified yield or swap rate). [Article 4(4)][model] requires an appropriate pricing model for gamma and vega in the non-delta capital calculation.
