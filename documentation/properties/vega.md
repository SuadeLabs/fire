# vega

---

The **vega** attribute measures the sensitivity of the derivative's value to a change in the volatility of the underlying risk factor and is a numeric field.

For options and warrants, [Regulation (EU) 528/2014, Article 4(1)(b)][vega] identifies the sensitivity to implied volatility. [Article 4(4)][model] requires an appropriate pricing model for gamma and vega in the non-delta capital calculation.
